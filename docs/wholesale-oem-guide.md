# Wholesale & OEM Guide

How to buy FPV hardware in volume, or develop a custom-branded product line, from [FPV Supply Co](https://fpvsupplyco.com).

---

## Who This Guide Is For

- **Distributors & retailers** expanding an FPV catalog
- **FPV brands** launching their own product line
- **Drone startups** moving from prototype to production
- **Project buyers** sourcing hardware for industrial, agricultural, or educational deployments

---

## 1. Ways to Work With Us

| Mode | What You Get | Typical Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| **Component Supply** | Off-the-shelf FPV parts, single units or bulk | Builders, repair shops, small retailers |
| **Wholesale** | Tiered bulk pricing on catalog products | Distributors, retailers |
| **OEM** | Your brand on existing hardware | FPV brands, retailers with own labels |
| **ODM** | Custom design + manufacturing | Startups, project buyers needing custom platforms |
| **Custom Integration** | Complete assembled platforms for a specific application | Industrial, agriculture, SAR, education |

---

## 2. The Order Flow

**Step 1 — Tell us what you need**
Product or platform, target quantity, target market, any branding or spec requirements.

**Step 2 — Quotation**
We come back with unit pricing, MOQ, lead time, and shipping terms based on volume.

**Step 3 — Sample order**
For any volume commitment, we recommend a sample order first. Evaluate the hardware hands-on before scaling.

**Step 4 — Confirmation & production**
Production scheduling, custom configuration or branding applied as agreed.

**Step 5 — Quality control**
Testing and inspection before shipment. For FC/ESC stacks this includes firmware verification and board function checks to reduce dead-on-arrival rate.

**Step 6 — Shipping**
Consolidated logistics, export documentation, tracking through to delivery.

---

## 3. Volume Pricing Logic

Wholesale pricing is tiered by quantity. As a general principle:

| Quantity Band | Pricing Tier |
|---|---|
| Sample / evaluation | List price (or sample price) |
| Small batch | Entry wholesale |
| Standard wholesale | Mid-tier |
| Container / production volume | Best tier, negotiable |

Exact tiers and MOQ depend on the specific product — off-the-shelf components have far lower MOQ than custom-configured or custom-branded items. [Request a quote](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/contact-us) with your target quantity for real numbers.

---

## 4. Custom Branding (OEM)

Options available, depending on product and volume:

- **Logo on product** — silkscreen or laser marking on frames, motors, boards
- **Custom packaging** — your branding on boxes and inserts
- **Custom firmware/preset** — shipped with your preferred tune or configuration
- **Custom spec** — changed components, different connector, altered layout

Branding options and minimum volumes vary by product category. The higher the customization, the higher the MOQ.

---

## 5. Custom Development (ODM)

For applications that off-the-shelf hardware cannot cover:

- **Concept development** — working from your application requirements
- **Prototype** — functional prototype for testing and iteration
- **Design freeze** — locking the spec
- **Production** — scalable manufacturing runs
- **Documentation** — spec sheets and support material for your customers

Typical applications we support include industrial inspection platforms, agricultural monitoring hardware, search & rescue configurations, cinematic FPV systems, and educational drone kits. See our [manufacturing capabilities](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/about-us-manufacturing-capabilities).

---

## 6. Quality Assurance

What we check before shipment:

- Firmware version and function verification
- ESC calibration and board function testing
- Visual inspection for soldering and assembly defects
- Packaging integrity for international transit

**Why this matters:** dead-on-arrival hardware is the most expensive kind of defect for a retailer. It costs the unit, the shipping both ways, and customer trust. Pre-shipment testing directly reduces this.

---

## 7. Shipping & Logistics

- **Worldwide shipping** with global logistics support
