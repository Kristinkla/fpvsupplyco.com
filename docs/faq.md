Frequently asked questions about ordering, shipping, compatibility, and support at [FPV Supply Co](https://fpvsupplyco.com).

## Ordering

**Do you sell to individual builders, or only to businesses?**
Both. We supply single components to individual builders and bulk orders to distributors, brands, and project buyers. [Browse the catalog](https://fpvsupplyco.com/collections/all).

**Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**
Off-the-shelf products generally have low or no MOQ. MOQ applies mainly to custom-configured and custom-branded items, since these require production setup. [Ask us](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/contact-us) about your specific product.

**Can I order a sample before committing to volume?**
Yes, and we recommend it. Sample orders let you evaluate hardware hands-on before scaling. Standard practice for any volume purchase.

**How do I get a wholesale price?**
Submit your product list and target quantity through the [quote request form](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/contact-us). Pricing is tiered by volume.

**Do you offer OEM / custom branding?**
Yes — logo marking, custom packaging, custom firmware presets, and spec changes, depending on product and volume. [Details in the Wholesale & OEM Guide](wholesale-oem-guide.md).

**Can you develop a fully custom drone platform?**
Yes. We support concept through production for industrial inspection, agriculture, search & rescue, cinematic, and educational applications. See our [manufacturing capabilities](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/about-us-manufacturing-capabilities).

## Products & Compatibility

**How do I know which FC stack fits my build?**
Match the stack's mounting pattern and UART count to your frame and peripherals. Our [FPV Component Selection Guide](fpv-component-selection-guide.md) walks through this.

**Which ESC rating do I need for my motors?**
Size the ESC 20–30% above your motors' peak current draw. Specific pairings are covered in the [selection guide](fpv-component-selection-guide.md#3-esc-match-the-current-rating-to-the-motors).

**What motor KV should I choose?**
KV needs to match your battery cell count. 6S freestyle typically wants 1700–1900KV; 4S wants 2400–2600KV. Full table in the [selection guide](fpv-component-selection-guide.md#4-motors-kv-is-the-whole-story).

**Do the FC stacks come with firmware installed?**
Yes. Stacks ship with firmware installed and ESC calibration verified as part of our pre-shipment QC.

**Are the products genuine branded hardware?**
Products are listed with their vendor. For example, our GEPRC stacks are GEPRC hardware. Vendor is shown on each product page.

**Can you source a part that is not in the catalog?**
Often yes. Open an [Issue](../../issues) with the part or specification you need — we will check availability.

**What does "AIO" mean, and should I buy one?**
AIO means the FC and ESC are combined on one board. Good for tight builds like whoops and sub-250g drones. Repairability is lower since a single failure means replacing the whole board.

## Quality & Support

**What if a product arrives dead on arrival (DA)?**
Contact us with your order details and photos/video of the issue. Pre-shipment testing minimizes this, but if it happens, we will work with you on resolution.

**What quality checks do you perform before shipping?**
Firmware verification, ESC calibration, board function testing, visual inspection for solder and assembly defects, and packaging integrity checks.

**What if a part fails after I have flown it?**
Crash damage and wear are not covered, but manufacturing defects are. Contact us with details and we will assess.

**Do you provide technical support for builds?**
Yes — for component selection, compatibility questions, and sourcing. Ask here via [Issues](../../issues) or through the [contact form](https://fpvsupplyco.com/pages/contact-us).
