## What We Do

### 🔧 Component Sourcing
Direct access to FPV manufacturing resources. Single units for evaluation, bulk quantities for production. We help you match the right FC stack, ESC rating, motor KV, and frame geometry to your build target — instead of guessing from spec sheets.

### 🏭 OEM / ODM Manufacturing
From prototype development to scalable production. Custom branding, custom specifications, custom packaging. We support FPV brands launching their own product lines with reliable hardware backing.

### 📦 Wholesale Supply
Competitive wholesale pricing for distributors and retailers looking to expand their FPV catalog. Tiered pricing by volume, consolidated shipping, and stable restock cycles.

### ✅ Quality Assurance & Certification
Testing and inspection before shipment. We verify firmware, ESC calibration, and board function on FC/ESC stacks prior to dispatch, so your customers do not receive dead-on-arrival hardware.

### 🚚 Worldwide Shipping
Global logistics support for business buyers, including consolidated shipments and export documentation for engineering projects.
